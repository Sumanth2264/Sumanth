CinePing Privacy

CinePing is a movie-alert website. The current GitHub Pages version stores alert preferences and demo watchlists in your browser using local storage.

Email

If email delivery is enabled in a future backend, your email address will be used only to create and deliver the alerts you request. CinePing will not ask for your email password.

Location

The website does not require precise location. If you choose browser location access, it is used for nearby-cinema sorting in the browser.

Third-party providers

Booking is completed on official provider websites. CinePing does not collect payment-card details.
